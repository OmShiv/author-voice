# Starter profile

This is a preference preset derived from the supplied prompt, not an inferred profile of the author's writing. Copy it into a project's instructions if useful; adapt it separately for each book or paper.

Write with technical precision, clarity, and understatement for an informed reader. Prefer concrete nouns and operational verbs. Remove promotional judgments and transitions that add no logical relationship. Retain familiar disciplinary language, meaningful uncertainty, and enough explanation for the intended audience.

Keep sentence rhythm responsive to the thought without imposing a length pattern. Use active voice for known author decisions and passive voice where the procedure or result deserves emphasis. Preserve established person and tense.

Prefer commas, parentheses, or separate sentences to em-dashes in ordinary prose. Preserve dashes in quotations, established terms, ranges, and mathematical expressions. Treat inflated vocabulary as a reason to inspect the sentence, not a forbidden-word list.

Discuss constraints, overhead, failures, and tradeoffs only when the source provides them. Never manufacture a sequence of failed experiments. Keep numbers, units, equations, citations, quotations, code, and substantive qualifications intact.
