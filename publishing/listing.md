# Author Voice

Natural manuscript prose with evidence preserved.

Author Voice helps you draft and revise research papers and books in your own voice. Use it while drafting, or edit an existing section before publication. It focuses on clear, specific language while preserving technical detail, citations, uncertainty, and the scope of your claims.

Choose a light revision, an editorial audit, or a reusable voice profile based on your own writing samples. Guidance for research papers and books supports the different needs of each genre. An optional offline checker highlights changes to selected numbers, units, citations, quotations, equations, and code for review.

Author Voice runs inside your AI platform. It does not operate a separate rewriting service, require a publisher-issued API key, or collect manuscripts through its own backend. Document editing and export use capabilities available in your AI platform.

AI-assisted revisions still need author review. Author Voice does not guarantee factual correctness, semantic equivalence, a detector outcome, or publication acceptance.
